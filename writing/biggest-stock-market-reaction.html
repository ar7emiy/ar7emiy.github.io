Biggest Stock Market Reaction in 5 years - An analysis using python

Biggest Stock Market Reaction in 5 years

An analysis using python

After seeing stock market perform so poorly, I wanted to check how unique today, December 18th 2024, was historically.

I wrote a simple script in python using the yfinance library to check how strong the reaction to today's FOMC meeting was.

The results were interesting:

Today was the single largest jump in trading volatility in the last 5 years, since the first US Covid-19 death was recorded.

It is also the largest in the last 20 years, second only to the Feb 27, 2018 4.6% market drop and largest ever drop in Dow Index.

Today's volatility jump was higher than when Iran attacked US bases in middle east earlier this summer. Even higher than the February 27, 2007, when the Housing market crash was becoming imminent.

It is pretty clear that the market took Jerome Powell's plan to heart. I wonder what this means for the future?
