About

I'm a builder operating at the intersection of agentic AI and real-world operations. At Gallagher Bassett I lead AI solutions across claims and fraud — including the flagship fraud detection platform.

I don't do demos that die in notebooks. I take systems from zero to production: entity resolution, graph architectures, eval harnesses, and agents that sit in front of real users doing real work.

I love prototyping, innovating, and learning deeply complex topics — but what I really enjoy is being forward deployed. And when I'm forward deployed on something that has to be highly innovative? I can't get enough of it.

I'm drawn to hard, regulated domains — the places where correctness is contractual and "the model can probably do this" isn't good enough. That's where the interesting problems live.
